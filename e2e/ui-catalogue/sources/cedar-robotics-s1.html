Cedar Robotics

Form S-1 · Preliminary prospectus excerpt

May 12, 2026

Proposed offering

We plan to offer shares of our Class A common stock to the public for the first time. We intend to list the shares under the symbol CDR. The number of shares and the initial public offering price have yet to be determined.

Our business

We design mobile robots that carry parts between workstations on factory floors. Customers buy the robots and subscribe to software that assigns routes and schedules charging.

Use of proceeds

We expect to use a portion of the proceeds to add a second assembly line and expand our field service team. We intend to use the balance for working capital.
