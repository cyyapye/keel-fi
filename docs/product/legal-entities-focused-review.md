# Legal entities: focused review

The user approved proposal V4 on 2026-10-14 and requested implementation and
release, including the earlier automatic-resolution scope. Holdings is the
invariant; its financial forms, calculations and source-link workflow stay
unchanged.

The ordinary task is to inspect a legal vehicle, retrieve its agreement or
correct its identity. Identity, Documents and Ownership remain the legal tabs.
Investment terms link to the exact position in Holdings using its existing
position URL. Old Investment-tab links use the same destination.

## Editing owners

- Holdings owns cash, refunds, shares, fees, carry, valuation, calculations and
  financial/source-link history.
- Legal identity and company corrections retain their existing versioned
  writers. Initial company assignment links to the existing Holdings workflow.
- Documents retains guarded downloads, source citations, all assignment targets
  and document-backed pending or cancelled investment creation.
- Ownership retains optional dated observations, K-1 evidence and amendments.
- Participation, closure/replacement and corporate events retain their existing
  writers under a secondary disclosure on Identity.
- Source commitment corrections remain a separate secondary action. They affect
  commitment and fee calculations, not actual cash paid, refunds, funded capital
  or valuation. Treating them as the purchase cash editor would lose behavior.
- Counsel export and company-name/alias review remain at their existing entry
  points.
- The explicit same-investment/rename exception link retains its existing
  keep-record chooser, suggestions and identity gates. Its shared continuity
  editor opens only for that requested exception, never as a second editor on
  the ordinary legal record. Ordinary source-link management stays in Holdings.

## Automatic decisions

The bounded authenticated resolver continues to record decisions. It can reuse a
unique full-position company mapping accepted by the exact reviewed-company
policy when the current source still exactly matches the accepted company or an
approved alias. New conflicting agreement evidence remains a company exception.
A deal label alone never establishes a new mapping here. Legal vehicles and
provider positions are never merged by company name.

Optional financial issues remain recorded and unknown fees remain unknown. They
do not enter the Legal entities queue or require another legal review. Actual
identity, document and participation exceptions retain their boundaries.
Recovery remains outside GET requests, with no new polling or provider reads.

## Challenge and regressions

For a known entity with missing fees, opening the legal record requires no
financial input. A company correction and agreement download remain reachable
after automatic resolution. Multiple SPVs use their exact position links;
matching companies do not combine their values or identity.

An incomplete legal name still requires evidence. Blocked or ambiguously
assigned agreements remain reviewable and cannot gain an unsafe download. Save,
reload, later correction and rejected-draft coverage exercise the reused
writers. A 120-position D1 holdout covers fixed round trips, growing tied-date
history, unrelated households and write-free repeated recovery.

The old assertions requiring a legal Investment tab, fee editor or fee-only
review queue must fail. Existing Holdings screenshots and interactions must
continue to pass without baseline changes.
